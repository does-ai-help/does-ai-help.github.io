## What the Review Should Ask

- **New feasibility:** can AI make previously uneconomic modernization worthwhile? This is a substantive hypothesis, distinct from accelerating an unchanged workload. Neither enthusiasm nor the absence of completed results settles it.
- **Define the denominator:** what qualifies as legacy code, and is coverage measured by lines, repositories, services or business functions? A percentage alone cannot establish value.
- **Define completion:** distinguish generated edits, reviewed changes, successful migration and sustained production operation. Refactoring should preserve specified behavior; a rewrite may require different acceptance criteria.
- **Count the whole system:** include testing, security review, domain-expert support, operational migration, rollback preparation, infrastructure and maintenance. A small coding team need not imply an equally small total labor input.
- **Treat spending as an experiment:** compare incremental benefits with total costs and define milestones, failure criteria and stop conditions. Neither generous access nor spending restraint is intrinsically optimal.
- **Avoid demographic inference:** a selected success story cannot estimate hiring demand, graduate opportunities or generation-wide aptitude. Test whether any observed advantage transfers to ordinary teams and training pathways.

## Proposed Article Passage

Large modernization proposals belong in a forward-looking case register rather than the pool of demonstrated productivity gains. They make the possible expansion of feasible work concrete, while leaving the decisive questions open: accepted functionality, reliability, total resource requirements and durable business value. Evaluation should preserve both possibilities: a genuine discontinuity in what small teams can accomplish, or substantial downstream costs hidden by a compelling announcement. [@roylecisco2026]

## Follow-Up Protocol

At release preparation, seek dated milestone results, a defined scope, independent customer or engineering corroboration, production-quality measures and cost disclosure. Record negative results, delays and abandoned scope as carefully as successful deployments. If these remain unavailable, retain the case as a proposal rather than silently upgrading its evidentiary status. No alternative Chrome verification was needed for this intake.
