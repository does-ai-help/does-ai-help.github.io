## Evidence and Boundaries

The survey recruited weekly workplace GenAI users through Prolific. Its 319 respondents supplied 936 retained examples. Models account for repeated observations within people; these examples are not independent participants. Higher task-specific confidence in AI is associated with less reported critical thinking, while confidence in one's own ability has the opposite association. Qualitative findings describe verification, integration and stewardship. [@ref-712e3405f2]

The authors acknowledge possible confusion between reduced effort and reduced thinking. This is not a longitudinal skill-loss study or an objective performance test. Self-confidence is not measured expertise; English-speaking regular users are not all workers. Most coauthors' Microsoft Research affiliation merits disclosure, not dismissal. [@ref-712e3405f2]

## Interpretation for the Review

**Separate the outcomes.** Effort expended, quality of reasoning, immediate output, retained ability and transfer to an unfamiliar task are distinct. Improvement in one can coexist with deterioration in another. Measuring only perceived effort cannot adjudicate all five.

**Do not turn an association into a treatment effect.** Task suitability, expertise, tool choice and remembered experiences are plausible influences. A regression can address specified covariates without removing all selection or reverse-direction explanations.

**Do not equate oversight with either mastery or empty ritual.** Verification may demand substantial domain expertise; it can also become superficial. Test what people detect and correct rather than infer engagement from a job description or an assurance that someone remains responsible.

**Keep competing explanations open.** Less effort may indicate efficient delegation, insufficient scrutiny, or both across different tasks. A potential long-term risk deserves investigation without being presented as an already measured decline.

## Proposed Article Passage

Evidence about cognition requires precision about what changed. Self-reported effort is informative about workers' experience, but it is not interchangeable with reasoning quality or retained skill. Studies should combine process measures with immediate performance, delayed unaided assessment and transfer tests. Such distinctions allow the review to recognize both useful cognitive relief and the risk of weakened independent capability without presuming either outcome. [@ref-712e3405f2]
