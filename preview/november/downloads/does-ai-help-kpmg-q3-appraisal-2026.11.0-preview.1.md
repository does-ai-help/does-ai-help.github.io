## Interpretation

These are executive self-reports, not causal effects or audited profits. Eligibility starts at US$50 million revenue, rises to US$100 million in specified markets, and reaches US$1 billion for the US tracking sample (page 25). Value/cost questions exclude early-stage respondents (page 22); 12% does not imply that everyone else never evaluates value. Maturity categories incorporate ROI, making comparisons selection-sensitive. Raw data, respondent continuity and detailed weighting are not established here. KPMG's commercial interest should remain visible. AI-assisted analysis and editing are disclosed (page 25).

The source improves primary-document coverage and cost-accounting context; it does not establish an economy-wide productivity gain. The downloaded PDF's checksum is recorded in the evidence database; it is not redistributed.
